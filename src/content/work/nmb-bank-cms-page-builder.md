## Situation

NMB Bank is a Class-A commercial bank licensed by Nepal Rastra Bank, with 197 branches and 2.2M+ customers (per [nmb.com.np](https://www.nmb.com.np/en)). The bank needed a public site where marketing and content teams could update pages, menus, and product content without engineering tickets — in English and Nepali.

## What I owned

- **CMS-driven public frontend** on Next.js (App Router) + React: pages, menus-as-data, bilingual routes, and media rendered from the admin CMS
- **Drag-and-drop page builder** for internal pages: reusable blocks with typed schemas and editor fields, saved as structured JSON and rendered by the same components as the live site
- Guardrails so non-developers can compose pages without breaking brand or layout constraints

## Constraints

Regulated brand, bilingual content (EN/NE), content editable end-to-end from an admin panel, and a public site credited to Peace Nepal DOT Com in the footer.
