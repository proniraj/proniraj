## Situation

Marketing teams at Hourmarket needed to change landing pages, email templates, and mobile screens regularly. Every update went through engineering, which slowed campaigns and tied up the team. Customers also needed a native shopping experience on phones.

## What I worked on

- Visual page builder across **web, mobile screens, and email** templates
- Consumer **React Native shopping app** on iOS and Android — product browsing, carts, and checkout flows in the same TypeScript stack as the web products
- Backend migration from **.NET to NestJS** for services that were hard to deploy and scale
- **Unified authentication** shared across Hourmarket web and mobile surfaces

## Outcome

Non-technical teams could launch campaigns on three surfaces without filing engineering tickets for each change. Shoppers got a native mobile app; backend migration made deploys faster across multiple services.

## Stack

React, TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, React Native (iOS / Android)
