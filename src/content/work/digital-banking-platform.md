## Situation

A Nepali financial institution needed customers to open and manage accounts without branch visits. Manual onboarding was slow for both the bank and customers.

## What I worked on

- **Digital onboarding** flows — verification, account setup, activation
- **Account management** workflows customers could complete online
- Direct work with client stakeholders on a fixed timeline

## Outcome

Reduced manual onboarding steps and shortened the time from signup to an active account. Delivered alongside two other client products during the same engagement at Basework.io.

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Node.js
