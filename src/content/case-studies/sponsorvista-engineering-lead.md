## Context

Sponsorvista is a sponsorship platform scaling quickly with a distributed remote engineering team. The business needed consistent delivery without sacrificing reliability for users already in production.

## The problem

- Growing platform with pressure to ship features quickly
- Distributed team needed clear execution standards
- Production users depended on uptime and performance
- Deployments and debugging couldn't be ad hoc anymore

## My role

Engineering lead and full stack contributor — owning execution across the team, shipping features myself, and raising standards for reviews, deployments, and reliability.

## What I owned

- End-to-end feature delivery serving **active production users**
- **Deployments, debugging, and infrastructure** — not just code
- **Code review standards** and practices that raised team velocity
- **Page-load performance** and platform reliability improvements

## Impact

- Shipped production features used by **active customers**
- Improved **page-load performance** and **deployment reliability**
- Established engineering practices that scaled with a **distributed remote team**

## What I'd tell a hiring manager

I'm comfortable wearing two hats: **hands-on builder** and **person who unblocks a team**. In startup environments, that combination is often what founders actually need.
