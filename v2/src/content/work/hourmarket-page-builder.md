## Situation

Marketing teams at Hourmarket needed to change landing pages, email templates, and mobile screens regularly. Every update went through engineering, which slowed campaigns and tied up the team.

## What I worked on

- Visual page builder across **web, mobile, and email** templates
- Backend migration from **.NET to NestJS** for services that were hard to deploy and scale
- **Unified authentication** shared across Hourmarket products
- Consumer **mobile shopping app** on iOS and Android

## Outcome

Non-technical teams could launch campaigns on three surfaces without filing engineering tickets for each change. Backend migration made deploys faster across multiple services.

## Stack

React, TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, React Native
